---
title: "Recall and promote knowledge"
description: "Read back what this project already learned, and lift a hard-won lesson into every project."
---

Run `/flow recall` at the start of a stage or a card, before you author anything. It reads
back the durable layer — open debt, the most recent retro, the previous card's scope, harness
friction and backlog, audit health, and any promoted playbooks — so the work starts with
prior pain in view instead of rediscovering it. When a lesson turns out to be bigger than one
project, `/flow promote <playbook.md>` copies it into the cross-project knowledge base at
`~/.claude/flow/playbooks`, and from then on `recall` surfaces it everywhere rather than only
where it was learned. The loop is capture, reuse, improve: `next` and `check` write records
automatically, `recall` reads them back, and `harness propose` mines repeated friction into an
improvement backlog.
