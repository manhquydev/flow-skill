## Stage 00 — Idea
Mechanical: pitch present, one real person named, no FILL.
**Challenge:** Is the pitch really 3 sentences (who / pain / what)? Is the named person a
*real, specific* person or group ("my uncle's 20-unit building"), not "people who..." or
"users"? Is the pain concrete, not a category?
- **Offer the forge-idea ritual (opt-in-with-prompt — `native-rituals.md`'s sibling
  `references/forge-idea.md`).** Before drafting the pitch, or when the operator sounds
  unsure, offer to pressure-test the idea through persona interrogation until it
  hardens, proves out, or dies cheaply. Never auto-fired; never a condition this gate
  checks for — a killed idea and a hardened idea both still go through the normal gate.

### Brownfield inspection prerequisite
For a `00-inspect.md` assessment, apply this challenge instead of the idea pitch
challenge above. An existing assessment must be clean before planning unlocks or
continues, including when stages 00–05 already exist. An absent assessment is
greenfield, not a missing prerequisite. An explicit skip must name `00-inspect`
and have an open DEBT naming that same stage; unrelated debt is not permission.
**Challenge:** Does the assessment show what was inspected, with concrete source
paths/symbols and observed behavior supporting its readiness verdict? FLAG an
unchecked inspection, unsupported readiness claim, or a checked-box assertion
without evidence. PASS a completed inspection whose evidence substantiates the
verdict, or a documented stage-matched DEBT skip with its exposure and close-before
condition. Do not mistake later planning files for evidence that inspection passed.
